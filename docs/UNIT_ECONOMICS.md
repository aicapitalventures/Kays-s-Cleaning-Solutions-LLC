# Pricing & Unit Economics v1.0 — FOUNDER DRAFT
Systems Architecture & Development: Elijah L. Cooley | Divinityx Enterprises, LLC.

Contribution per job = net collected service revenue − fully loaded field labor − job supplies − travel − transaction fees − other variable job expenses.
Profit also requires subtracting insurance, marketing, management compensation, software, equipment, taxes where applicable and other fixed costs.

Quote model: estimated productive person-hours × true hourly labor burden + materials + route expense + allocated overhead + target profit. Add explicitly approved surcharges based on documented scope; test quoted time against actual. Never present placeholder margins or market rates as validated.

KPIs: booked cost of acquisition, revenue per labor hour, job contribution, re-clean rate, repeat-booking rate, monthly cash collection and effective acquisition payback.
