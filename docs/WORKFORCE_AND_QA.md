# Workforce, Dispatch & QA v1.0 — FOUNDER DRAFT
Systems Architecture & Development: Elijah L. Cooley | Divinityx Enterprises, LLC.

Recruit qualified cleaners under a lawfully reviewed worker model. Collect necessary employment and eligibility records in secure systems, not GitHub. Use consent-compliant background screening, role-specific training, safety and chemical handling instruction, PPE, customer privacy practices and incident reporting.

Before dispatch verify cleaner availability, expected hours, supplies, transport, service limitations and customer-approved scope. Track acceptance, arrival, departure, completed room-level checklist and deviations. Never expose door/access codes in plaintext alerts. Only capture site photographs with appropriate consent.

Escalate safety risks, property damage, theft claims, complaints, missed shifts and re-clean requests. Maintain reserve cleaner coverage and a documented service-recovery budget/authority.
