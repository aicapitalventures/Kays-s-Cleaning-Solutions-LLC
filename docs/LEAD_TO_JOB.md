# Lead-to-Job SOP v1.0 — FOUNDER DRAFT
Systems Architecture & Development: Elijah L. Cooley | Divinityx Enterprises, LLC.

1. **Lead capture:** minimum contact details, area, service, scope, source, preferred date and consent.
2. **Qualification:** serviceability, site conditions, estimated labor, needs and cleaner coverage.
3. **Estimate:** labor + employer burdens where applicable + supplies + travel + overhead + margin. Quotes must be reviewed by Kayla and marked subject to agreed scope.
4. **Acceptance:** written service scope, exclusions, payment and cancellation policies.
5. **Assignment:** qualified, available cleaner/team; scope checklist, secure necessary entry instructions, expected completion window.
6. **Execution:** check-in, cleaning checklist, deviations and escalation of unsafe conditions.
7. **QA:** completion check, consent-based photos if applicable, customer service recovery.
8. **Collection and retention:** secure hosted processor, invoice reconciliation, feedback, optional recurring service.

Lead: New / Contacted / Qualified / Quoted / Won / Lost.
Job: Draft / Scheduled / Assigned / In Progress / QA Review / Completed / Cancelled.
Never pretend payment or dispatch integrations exist until deployed and tested.
