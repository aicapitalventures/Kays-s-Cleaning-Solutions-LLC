# Managed Cleaning Operating Model v1.0
**FOUNDER DRAFT — not a live service.**
Systems Architecture & Development: Elijah L. Cooley | Divinityx Enterprises, LLC.

## Operator
Kayla Trautvetter manages lead generation, customer intake, estimates, cleaner staffing, dispatch, service-level oversight, collections and retention. Field crews perform client-site cleaning. Owner coverage includes exceptions, safety and escalations. This is an intended management-led model, not an assertion that any staff are currently hired.

## Service segments
Residential recurring standard clean, deep clean, move-in/out, property turnover, small offices and commercial service contracts. Qualify every special job before pricing (hazards, post-construction, biohazard, specialty restoration excluded until separately qualified and insured).

## Lifecycle
Ad → inquiry → qualify → site scope → estimated quote → customer acceptance → capacity confirmation → schedule/assign → job checklist → QA → invoice/payment → feedback → recurring offer.

## Metrics
Response time; leads by source; contact rate; quote acceptance; revenue/job; productive labor hours; fully loaded field labor; material/travel cost; job contribution; acquisition cost per won customer; on-time completion; complaints/recleans; repeat bookings; customer retention.

## Controls
No automatic acceptance of jobs; no availability promise before staffing; least-privilege handling of client access instructions; verify occupational safety, appropriate insurance and worker classification; maintain complaint and incident playbooks.
